How to read

Balls are pools of money: cash, bonds, real assets, stocks, crypto, from safest (left) to riskiest (right). Size = dollars held there.

Streams are money that actually moved, measured by the Federal Reserve's Financial Accounts (Z.1): net purchases by US households, foreign investors and the Fed. Cyan flows into a pool; amber flows out of it. Width = dollars per quarter. Stablecoin minting is measured weekly.

Thin lines show which prices moved together (blue) or opposite (orange) over the last 13 weeks. Moving together is not cause.

The timeline stacks every pool's size over 20 years; the lines under it are the drivers (inflation, rates, oil, dollar). Dots mark the biggest 4-week moves.

A ball grows either because its price rose or because money flowed in. The streams separate the two. Values between quarterly releases, the gold stock and the bitcoin supply are estimates and are marked so. Korean pool sizes need a Bank of Korea ECOS key.
